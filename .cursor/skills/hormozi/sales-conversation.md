# Sales conversation

The offer closes. The conversation makes the buyer say the problem out loud, believe this vehicle solves it, and decide. Write talk tracks in spoken words. Short. One question at a time.

## Before the call

Know the one-sentence offer, the price, the guarantee, and the downsell. If any of those are mush, go back to [offers.md](offers.md). A script on a mushy offer sounds like pressure.

## The six beats

Use their name. Let them talk. Do not pitch in beat 1.

1. **Clarify.** Why this conversation, why now. "What made you take the call?" "What are you hoping happens if this works?"
2. **Label.** Repeat the problem in their words. One sentence. Wait. "So the business does $X, and you are stuck because Y."
3. **Overview the past.** What they tried, what it cost, why it failed. This builds the cost of staying the same and stops you from pitching what they already hate.
4. **Sell the vacation.** The result and the first two weeks. Not the module list. Tie each piece of the stack to a pain they just said. "You said ads eat cash before a customer pays you back. That is why week one is the offer and the 30-day cash map, not a brand workshop."
5. **Explain the concerns.** Ask "What would stop you from doing this?" Handle the real one. Then ask if anything else is in the way. See below.
6. **Reinforce.** Recap the result, the price, the guarantee, and the next physical step with a time. "You start Monday. You send me the last 20 sales calls by tomorrow noon. First working session is Tuesday."

## Objections

Isolate first: "Besides that, is anything else in the way?" Handle one.

- **Price.** Restate the cost of the unsolved problem in their numbers. If they believe the result and still cannot pay, offer the payment plan or the feature downsell from [money-models.md](money-models.md). Do not invent a secret discount.
- **Spouse or partner.** Agree. Offer a three-line summary they can forward, or a second call with the partner. Do not coach them to "sell the spouse" behind their back.
- **I need to think.** Ask what part is unclear: the result, the fit, or the money. Thinking is usually one of those. If they will not say, book a decision time and send the one-pager. Chasing is not a tactic.
- **I tried this before.** Ask what was different about the vehicle, the effort, and the guarantee. Show the specific difference. If there is no difference, do not sell it.
- **Send me info.** Info is a delay. Ask which question the info needs to answer. Answer it. Then ask for the decision.

## Notes they should leave with

After you draft a talk track, give Ivan a one-page leave-behind he can paste:

```
Result:
Who it is for:
What they do / what you do:
Time to first win:
Price and what is due today:
Guarantee:
Not a fit if:
Next step:
```

## What good sounds like

Direct, calm, specific. No false urgency. No "only three spots" unless there are three spots. No insulting the buyer for hesitating. The pressure comes from the math of their problem, not from your tone.
